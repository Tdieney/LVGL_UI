## Final generation prompt

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create one exceptional, professionally art-directed SMART HOME CONTROL HUB dashboard, brand-new composition. This is free creative redesign, NOT a recoloring of any prior screen. Output only the full straight-on screen, 1600x960 pixels (800x480 logical), no device bezel, poster, explanations, collage or external title.
Design concept: QUIET ARCHITECTURE. Sophisticated contemporary architectural home controls, warm stone background #F1F0EA, ink #202824, deep forest green #183D34 and luminous pale celadon #D8E8B7 accent. Warm porcelain surfaces, exceptionally precise editorial typography in a refined modern humanist sans (Plus Jakarta Sans-like), measured whitespace, optical alignment, quiet fine dividers, consistent outline icons. Gentle tonal gradient on the hero is welcome. Not a cartoon, not neon gamer, not an enterprise admin template. Natural contrast between a single dark feature panel and light functional surfaces. Clean deliberate visual hierarchy, neither uniformly white nor overwhelmingly dark.
Design for a small 800x480 touchscreen, not a scaled-down desktop website: all core readings and four device switches visible without scrolling, most text18-22 logical px, prominent values40-52px, large comfortable controls.
Structure:
A compact top header: left "Studio" in strong32px type with a tiny understated house mark; right stopwatch "00:24:18" followed by a signal indicator and "Connected". Time is uptime, not wallclock.
Below header, a compact 3-item horizontal navigation with readable labels "Overview", "Trends", "Devices". Overview selected as a dark-ink text on a pale celadon rounded capsule, others plain. No bottom nav, no sidebar, no additional app pages.
Main content begins near logical y120 and ends y456. Asymmetric two-column bento composition, 24px outer margins and16px gutter: LEFT width424, RIGHT width312.
LEFT top: a deep forest hero panel ~424x216 with24px inner padding and 20px radius. Top small label "Air quality", elegant simple airflow glyph at right. Large headline "Good" in cream, modest green check mark. Under headline short one-line supporting text "Your room feels fresh." Below, two aligned data columns elegantly integrated into panel, separated by a subtle vertical rule: "CO₂" with "420 ppm"; "VOC" with "85". Numbers big and white, units smaller. No invented aggregate score, dial, circle meter or percent health rating; the summary is illustrative for these good sample values.
LEFT bottom,16px gap: two equal porcelain tiles ~204x104, radius16, with label and black/forest outline pictogram at top and clear "23.5°C" and "48%" values below. Labels exactly "Temperature" and "Humidity". No extra invented comfort ratings.
RIGHT: one porcelain control panel312x336 radius20. Heading "Devices" and small settings/sliders icon. Four generous horizontal rows with subtle separators. Each row has a consistent elegant24px outline appliance icon at left, name and mode centrally, right-aligned54x30 switch within a generous tap area. Text rows18px readable. EXACT rows:
"Air purifier", "Auto", ON switch.
"Ventilation", "Auto", OFF switch.
"Humidifier", "Manual", OFF switch.
"Desk light", "Manual", ON switch.
Active switches deep forest with ivory thumb at right; inactive tracks stone gray with ivory thumb at left. Auto mode can have a small understated leaf glyph, not a second switch. Align toggles exactly. Do not add On/Off words.
Make it feel like a coherent premium physical home product, with purposeful surfaces and great typography, not a grid of four unrelated pastel metrics. All content fits, all text spelled correctly, realistic usable proportions. No people, photos, decoration charts, weather, multi-room selector, new appliances, logos, marketing CTAs or random tiny captions.
